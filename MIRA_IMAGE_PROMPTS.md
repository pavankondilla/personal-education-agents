# Mira sprite generation prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Reference: `assets/mira-companion.png`.

Each separate sheet has a 4 × 4 grid. Rows: idle, thinking, explaining, encouraging. The originals are retained in the generated-images library; project copies live in `assets/mira/`.

## teacher

Asset: `assets/mira/teacher-sheet.png`

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: production 2D animation sprite sheet for a premium educational web app. Image 1 is the Mira identity reference. Preserve Mira's cute rounded lavender body, large violet eyes, little curled tuft, star on forehead, mint wing-like ears, friendly face, and premium soft 3D toy rendering. Create a NEW outfit variant in a perfectly regular 4 columns by 4 rows sprite sheet, exactly 16 frames. Square canvas ideally 2048 by 2048 pixels, each of the 16 equal square cells has the same camera, size, center and baseline. One complete small full-body Mira in every cell, occupying 70% of cell width and height, generous transparent padding, no cropping, no overlap. Genuine transparent alpha background across whole canvas, no checkerboard, no floor, no cell borders, no letters, no labels, no watermark. Animation timing layout: row 1 four subtle idle/breathing frames (open eyes, half blink, closed blink, open eyes); row 2 four thoughtful frames (small head tilt, thoughtful glance, slight head tilt back, attentive); row 3 four explaining frames (small hand gesture and subtle mouth movement); row 4 four encouraging celebration frames (small wave, happy eyes, raised hands, warm smile). Maintain identical clothing and props through all 16 frames, readable silhouettes, consistent scale and lighting. Do not include another character.
Variant: TEACHER MIRA, warm, patient, reassuring. Dress Mira in a fitted cream knitted cardigan with lavender trim over a plum teacher's pinafore, small round gold-rimmed glasses, and hold a little closed coral textbook in one hand. Clothing should visibly wrap the rounded body without turning Mira into a human. Book has no text. Keep hands and friendly facial features readable. Gentle scholarly personality.
